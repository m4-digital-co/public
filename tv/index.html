Features

Everything you expect from a TV guide app. Nothing you don’t.

A proper programme guide

A full EPG grid from your XMLTV guide. See what is on now, what is next, and scroll ahead by the show.

Built for the remote

Every screen is designed for a D-pad. Browse, tune and back out without hunting for focus.

All your playlists, one lineup

Add several M3U or Xtream sources and they merge into one lineup. Filter by playlist or group any time.

Movies and shows

Your provider’s on-demand library with posters, details and resume where you left off.

Favourites and reminders

Pin the channels you actually watch. Hold OK on a future show to get a reminder when it starts.

Send a playlist to the TV

Share from your phone as a QR code, a link or a six-character TV code. Lock it with a passphrase if it holds a login.

Make it yours

Midnight, Graphite and Glass themes, plus adjustable text size for across-the-room reading.

How it works

Three steps, all from the couch.

  1. 1

    Install on your TV

    From Google Play or the App Store once we launch.

  2. 2

    Add your playlist

    Paste an M3U link or sign in with Xtream details. Add a guide URL if your provider has one.

  3. 3

    Watch

    Open the guide, pick a show, press OK. Down brings the guide back.

FAQ

Questions

Does it come with channels?

No. IPTV App is a player. It ships with no channels, playlists or subscriptions. You add a playlist from a provider you already use.

What playlists does it support?

M3U and M3U8 playlists, Xtream Codes logins, and XMLTV programme guides (including gzipped guides).

Which devices?

Android TV and Google TV first, then Apple TV. It also runs in a web browser for a quick look.

Can I try it without a playlist?

Yes. Add our demo playlist: invented channels and guide, with openly licensed films and footage.
