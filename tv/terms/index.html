IPTV App is a media player. It does not include, sell, host or recommend any channels, playlists, streams or subscriptions. You add your own playlists from sources you choose.

  • We don’t check, endorse or control third-party playlists or streams, and aren’t responsible for them.

Sending a playlist to a TV

  • When you send a playlist from a phone, it is encrypted on your phone so only the receiving TV can decrypt it.
  • It travels through a third-party public relay (by default ntfy.sh, or a server you choose in Settings). The relay only handles encrypted data. We don’t operate it and don’t receive or store your playlists.
  • To help protect you, each device looks up its own public IP address and country using Cloudflare, and the TV compares them. That information travels only inside the encrypted message.
  • Codes expire after 3 minutes. The TV always asks before adding anything.
