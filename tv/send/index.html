Your playlists on this phone. Tap one to send it.

    Private by design

    • Your playlist is encrypted in this browser so only your TV can open it. This page never sees the key.
    • It passes through a free public relay (ntfy.sh) that only ever sees scrambled data, delivered live and not stored.
    • Nothing is saved by us. Codes last 3 minutes. “Remember on this phone” keeps a copy in this browser only; you can forget it any time.
    • You’re responsible for the playlists you add. Terms.
